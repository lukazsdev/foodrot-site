Unchud

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Unchud ("Unchud: Stop being a chud" on the App Store and Google Play) is a habit app that helps you cut down on fast food. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and what you can do about it. Unchud is made by Velori Labs Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17529890), which is responsible for this data ("we", "us"). Questions: lukazsdev@gmail.com.

The short version

  • No sign-up and no account. Everything you log stays on your phone.
  • We don't run servers that store your logs, photos, cravings, coins or streaks. We never see them.
  • The app doesn't use your location.
  • Apple or Google handle payments, and RevenueCat manages subscriptions. We never see your payment details.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking across other apps or websites. We don't sell data.
  • Settings → Delete all my data erases everything the app stores, straight away.

What stays on your phone

Your answers and logs

During onboarding you tell the app how often you eat fast food, what a typical order costs, when cravings hit and why you want to quit. As you use it, you log home-cooked meals, earned treats and slips, the outcome of cravings, a daily energy check-in, the moves you do, and the coins you earn and spend. All of this is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone, and it is used only to show your streak, score, coins and money saved. It is not sent to us or anyone else.

Meal and water photos

If you add a photo to a home-cooked meal, the app copies it into its own private storage on your phone so it shows in your history. On iPhone, the app checks on the phone itself that the photo shows food (or, for the water move, a glass or bottle); nothing is uploaded for this. Photos are not uploaded anywhere. The app only asks for camera or photo access when you choose to add a photo.

Moves

You can earn coins with short exercises. On iPhone, the camera can count your reps: the video is analysed on your phone as you move, and it is never recorded, saved or uploaded. Walk, run and step moves read the step count from your iPhone's motion sensors (Motion & Fitness), also only on your phone. On Android, moves are count-alongs and use no camera or sensors.

Reminders

Reminder notifications (daily check-in, an evening nudge, milestones, the end of an unlock window) are scheduled on your phone. No notification server is involved and no push token is sent to us. You can turn reminders off in the app or in your phone's settings.

Home screen widget (iPhone)

The widget shows your streak, money saved and coins. The app shares those few numbers with the widget through storage on your phone that only Unchud and its widget can read.

Delivery app lock

On iPhone, the lock uses Apple's Screen Time framework. Apple gives the app only opaque tokens for the apps you pick, so neither we nor the app can see which apps they are, and they never leave your phone. iOS asks you before the app can use it.

On Android, the lock uses Android's Accessibility service, which you switch on yourself in your phone's settings after the app explains what it does. The service is told which app comes to the front, and the app uses that only to show its lock screen over the delivery apps you picked. It never reads what's on your screen, it doesn't keep a record of the apps you open, and nothing it sees leaves your phone. The list of apps on your phone is read only to show the picker, and the apps you pick are stored only on your phone. You can switch the service off at any time in Settings → Accessibility.

What leaves your phone

Your subscription

If you subscribe, the App Store or Google Play takes the payment. RevenueCat, our subscription provider, receives a random app user ID created on your phone, your purchase and subscription records from Apple or Google, and basic device information (device model, operating system, app version, country and IP address) so it can tell the app whether your subscription is active. We don't receive your name, Apple ID, Google account or payment details. Nothing you log in the app is sent to RevenueCat.

Backups you control

On iPhone, the app keeps a compressed copy of your progress in your own iCloud account (iCloud key-value storage), so it comes back if you delete the app or move to a new iPhone. On Android, the app's data is included in your phone's own Google backup if you have it switched on (meal photos are left out of the cloud copy). These copies are held by Apple or Google in your account, not by us, and we can't read them.

Technical data

When the app talks to RevenueCat, the App Store or Google Play, those services see your IP address, as with any internet connection, and keep it under their own policies.

Who processes data for us

  • RevenueCat: subscription management, in the United States.
  • Apple and Google process payments and app downloads under their own privacy policies.

RevenueCat processes data only to provide its service to us. Transfers outside the UK and EU are covered by standard contractual clauses.

Legal basis

If you're in the UK or EU: subscription data is processed to provide the app you asked for (contract) and to prevent fraud and keep purchases working (our legitimate interests). Everything else stays on your device or in your own backups and isn't processed by us at all.

How long it's kept

  • On your phone: until you use Delete all my data or delete the app. Your iCloud copy is erased by Delete all my data too; backups your phone makes to iCloud, Google or a computer are under your control.
  • Subscription records: kept by RevenueCat while needed to manage your subscription and to meet legal and accounting requirements.

Your choices and rights

Use Settings → Delete all my data in the app to erase your logs, photos, streaks, coins and settings from your phone immediately, or simply delete the app. You can cancel a subscription at any time in your App Store account settings or in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. For anything about subscription records, email lukazsdev@gmail.com. Because the app never asks who you are, we may need your App Store or Google Play receipt to find your records.

If you're in the UK or EU, you can also complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

Health information

Unchud is a habit and money tool, not a medical, nutrition or weight-loss service. It doesn't track weight or calories. If you pick "lose weight" as one of your reasons during setup, that choice is stored only on your phone, like your other answers; nothing about your weight is collected. If your relationship with food feels out of control, talking to a doctor or an eating disorder support service, such as Beat in the UK, can genuinely help.

Children

Unchud isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If the way the app uses data changes, for example if a future version adds optional accounts or location alerts, we'll update this page and the date at the top before that version ships.
