Foodrot

You have to earn your fast food.

Foodrot is a habit app for cutting down on fast food and takeaways. You earn coins by cooking at home, beating cravings and checking in, and spend them on a treat you planned for. Pip, your buddy, thrives when you cook and gets sluggish when the chains win, and a money jar shows what you didn't hand over.

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email lukazsdev@gmail.com. We usually reply within a couple of days.

Subscriptions are managed by the App Store. To cancel, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Restore Purchases is in the app under Settings.

Your logs live on your phone. To erase them, use Settings → Delete all my data in the app, or delete the app.
