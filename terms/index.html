What the app does

Unchud helps you eat less fast food. It can lock the delivery apps you pick until you've earned them. You log meals, do short exercises, ride out cravings with a short timer and challenge, earn in-app coins ("Aura") for cooking, moving and resisting, and spend them on unlock windows and treats you planned for. It shows a streak, a daily score and an estimate of the money you didn't spend on fast food.

Not medical advice

The app is a habit and motivation tool. It does not give medical, nutritional or weight-loss advice, and nothing in it is a diagnosis or treatment. Money-saved figures are estimates based on what you entered. Physical challenges suggested during a craving are optional; only do what is safe for you. If your relationship with food feels out of control, please talk to a doctor or an eating disorder support service, such as Beat in the UK.
